Ebenezer Aid Solutions - Site Plan

Empowering Children and Persons with Disabilities

Site Name

Ebenezer Aid Solutions

This name represents an organization dedicated to providing support, empowerment, and sustainable solutions for children and persons with disabilities. It reflects hope, restoration, and community upliftment.

Optional Domain: ebenezeraidsolutions.org

Site Purpose

The website serves as a platform to share information about Ebenezer Aid Solutions, its mission, programmes, and community impact. It provides opportunities for donations, volunteering, and raising awareness about supporting persons with disabilities in rural communities.

Scenarios

  • How can I donate or support Ebenezer Aid Solutions?
  • What programmes are available for children and persons with disabilities?
  • How can I volunteer and get involved in community activities?

Color Schema

#2e7d32 (Green): Used for headers and headings (represents growth and hope).

#f9a825 (Gold): Used for accents and highlights.

#f5f7fa (Light Gray): Used for background.

Typography

Merriweather: Used for headings (formal and professional look).

Roboto: Used for body text (clean and readable).
