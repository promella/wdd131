To fully realise our vision, the Marite Disability Centre needs renovations and essential infrastructure upgrades to ensure safety, accessibility, and effective programme delivery. Support is required for:  Facility renovations and accessibility improvements  Agricultural inputs and infrastructure (tools, fencing, irrigation systems, and starter inputs)  Basic furniture, learning, and training resources Your support directly contributes to restoring dignity, building independence, and strengthening long-term community resilience in a rural area where opportunities remain limited.
