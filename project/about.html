About Our Organisation

Ebenezer Aid Solutions is dedicated to improving the quality of life of children and persons with disabilities through integrated programmes that promote education, skills development, dignity, and self-reliance. Our work is centered at the Marite Disability Centre, which serves as a hub for supporting individuals and families facing significant socio-economic challenges in the Bushbuckridge region.

Our Mission

Enhancing the lives of children and persons with disabilities through education, empowerment, and sustainable community support programs.

Our Vision

A world where persons with disabilities are fully included, empowered, and able to live independent and dignified lives.

Our Values

Dignity

Respecting every individual regardless of ability or background.

Empowerment

Helping people gain skills and confidence for independence.

Inclusion

Ensuring everyone is part of community life and opportunities.

Sustainability

Creating long-term solutions that continue to benefit communities.
